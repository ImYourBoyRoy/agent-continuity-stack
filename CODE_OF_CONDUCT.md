# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must keep participation respectful, constructive,
and focused on improving the project. Technical disagreement is welcome;
personal attacks are not.

Expected behavior includes:

- giving actionable, evidence-based feedback;
- distinguishing confirmed facts from inference;
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints;
- protecting credentials, privacy, and confidential reports;
- accepting correction and documenting material mistakes;
- keeping automated and AI-assisted contributions reviewable.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, impersonation, credential exposure,
or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, release channels, and other public spaces where someone represents
the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate this
standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement
should be proportional, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid
exposing reporters or sensitive evidence.

Use GitHub's content-reporting features for conduct concerns. When a report also
contains a vulnerability, credential, or exploit detail, use the private
process in `SECURITY.md` instead of a public issue.
